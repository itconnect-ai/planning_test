## Brand & Style

This design system balances tactile specialty coffee hospitality with the rigorous, high-precision clarity of point-of-sale (POS) and inventory ERP software. It serves cafe operators, head baristas, and multi-location managers who navigate dense financial tables, quick-draw POS workflows, batch recipe costing, and rapid inventory tracking in fast-paced operational environments.

The visual ethos blends **Warm Craft Minimalism** with **Structured Density**:
- **Tactile Warmth:** Eliminates sterile, cold enterprise blues in favor of warm stone canvas foundations, deep roasted espresso typography, and golden caramel interactive cues.
- **Data Legibility & Precision:** Numerical clarity is paramount. Figures are styled with monospace tabular lining, clear visual hierarchy between base integers and metric units (`원`, `g`, `ml`, `개`), and unequivocal semantic signifiers.
- **Operational Calm:** High-contrast information chunking reduces visual fatigue during high-volume shifts and late-night store balance reconciliation.

## Colors

The palette directly mirrors the material realities of a modern cafe operation—parchment order dockets, roasted espresso beans, warmed milk, and botanical accents—calibrated strictly for WCAG AAA/AA readability across desktop monitors, back-office laptops, and tablet POS screens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C26D2B` - Roasted Caramel / Amber Amber):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, current active tabs, highlighted batch buttons, and dominant key metrics.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F` - Forest Canopy Green):** Denotes positive financial performance, target-beating sales margins, completed reconciliations, and fully stocked inventory levels.
- **Tertiary (`#BA2D3A` - Crimson Cherry / Rose Alert):** Indicates low stock warnings, out-of-stock items, operating cost surges, refund states, and immediate barista intervention alerts.
- **Neutral Surface & Text (`#201A18` - Dark Espresso):** Replaces harsh jet-black with a dense, warm-toned charcoal-espresso to reduce eye strain under cafe track lighting while maintaining high contrast.

### Background & Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF9F6` (Warm Rice Linen)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Table Base):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White)
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Muted Inset / Table Header):** `#F3EFEA` (Warm Oat Neutral)
- **Border Default:** `#E4DCD3` (Muted Warm Stone)
- **Border Focus / Subtle Divider:** `#CFC4B7` (Craft Paper Border)

## Typography

Inter serves as the primary system engine due to its exceptional tabular figures (`tnum`) and localized glyph compatibility with Korean body text (Pretendard fallback in production).

### Numerical Hierarchy Rules
- All currency, volumetric, and stock metrics must enable OpenType `tabular-nums` (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`).
- **Unit Hierarchy:** Numeric values use bold weights (`600` or `700`), while measurement markers (`원`, `g`, `ml`, `개`, `%`) step down by one visual size level, set in regular weight (`400`) and rendered in secondary muted neutral (`#685E57`).
- **Table Density:** Table body cells stick strictly to `num-table` (13px/600) and `body-sm` (12px/400) to keep multi-column inventories visible above the fold.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column fluid grid system geared towards dense dashboard panels, modal cash reconciliations, and tabular POS reports.

### Form Factors & Adaptation
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `margin-desktop` (2rem / 32px), `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem / 24px). Sidebar persists in an expanded fixed rail (260px). Multi-column tables allow split-screen recipe/inventory editing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px, POS Primary):** 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1rem / 16px). Sidebar collapses to an icon dock (72px). Metric cards stack into 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns, `margin` (1rem / 16px), `gutter` (0.75rem / 12px). Quick order tables collapse to stacked micro-cards with side-by-side key financial summaries.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Compact vertical rhythm minimizes vertical scroll on back-bar terminals.
- Data tables use tight vertical cell padding (`0.5rem` / 8px) with wide horizontal cell gutters (`0.875rem` / 14px) for rapid lateral reading across cost sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, using **Tonal Layering** combined with **Low-Contrast Warm Outlines** to provide tactile distinction without visual clutter.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FBF9F6`. Background for the main dashboard shell.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Work Tables, Grid Panels):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px border of `#E4DCD3`. A gentle contact shadow (`0 1px 2px rgba(32, 26, 24, 0.04)`) separates interactive panels from the background canvas.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Tooltips, Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` with border `#D5CAC0` and ambient warm shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(32, 26, 24, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Settlement Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with a full backdrop shield (`#201A18` at 40% opacity) and an elevated soft shadow (`0 12px 32px rgba(32, 26, 24, 0.14)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses a restrained **Soft** shape geometry (`roundedness: 1` = `0.25rem` / 4px base):

- **Data Inputs, Small Buttons, Table Badges:** `rounded` (4px / 0.25rem). Retains crisp structure in compact accounting columns.
- **Cards, Filter Bars, Data Panels:** `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem). Softens larger surfaces while framing dense financial lists cleanly.
- **Modals, Drawer Sheets:** `rounded-xl` (12px / 0.75rem). Gives overlay surfaces a refined, human touch.
- **Status Pills (매장/포장, 마감, 품절):** Handled with fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish categorical meta-tags from square data cells and action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (신규 발주, 정산 마감):** Background `#C26D2B`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border none. Hover: `#AA5D22`. Active: `#914E1B`. Height: 36px (compact) / 44px (POS touch target).
- **Secondary Action (내역 다운로드, 필터):** Background `#FFFFFF`, foreground `#201A18`, border `1px solid #E4DCD3`. Hover: `#F3EFEA`.
- **Destructive Action (발주 취소, 품절 처리):** Background `#FDF2F2`, foreground `#BA2D3A`, border `1px solid #F8D7DA`. Hover: `#BA2D3A`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Status Badges
Status indicators use explicit semantic tinting over 10% opacity backgrounds to prevent visual fatigue while keeping operational states distinct:
- **매장 (Dine-in):** Pill shape. Background `#F3EFEA`, text `#685E57`, border `1px solid #E4DCD3`.
- **포장 (Takeout):** Pill shape. Background `#FDF4ED`, text `#C26D2B`, border `1px solid #F5DCC8`.
- **실적 vs 예상 (Target Surpassed):** Background `#EDF7F2`, text `#2D6A4F`, border `1px solid #C2E3D2`. Includes an upward trend arrow (`▲`).
- **부족 재고 (Low Stock Alert):** Background `#FDF2F2`, text `#BA2D3A`, border `1px solid #F8D7DA`. Dot indicator pulse before the label.
- **마감 완료 (Settled/Closed):** Background `#EAE7E3`, text `#4D443E`, border `1px solid #D5CAC0`.

### Data Tables
- **Header:** Background `#F3EFEA`, text `#685E57`, 11px uppercase `label-sm`, bottom border `1px solid #D5CAC0`.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle hover state (`#FAF7F3`), border bottom `1px solid #EFEAE3`.
- **Cell Content:** Text aligns left, figures (`원`, `g`, `ml`) align right and use monospace tabular numerals. Units are rendered in regular weight muted typography immediately after the value (e.g., `12,500`**원**).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Base Input:** Height 38px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4DCD3`, text `#201A18`.
- **Focus State:** Border `#C26D2B`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(194, 109, 43, 0.15)`.
- **Helper Tooltips:** Neutral background `#201A18`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded 4px, font size 11px, triggered on hover/tap for tax rules and margin calculations.

### KPI Metric Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px border (`#E4DCD3`), 8px border radius.
- Includes a top-level category label, a dominant headline figure (`num-display`), and a bottom footer with delta pills comparing current figures against target estimates (e.g., `+8.4% vs 예상`).
